## Verdict

Cost: the active hours, output tokens, tool calls and human turns in the table above, five ratification sittings, and six ratified items in this graph amended and re-stamped for a rename, for a skill text, a script, two graphs of thirty-seven items and eight tests. Return: the coverage rule kept withheld tests from being forgotten; the check gate turned an unmeetable requirement into a change to the tool rather than a workaround; the ratification stamps turned a rename into a re-acceptance rather than a drift; and every number here comes from a command anyone can rerun at the commit named. Against that: two of nine defects were found by gates, two by the human, and five by the AI reading its own output, and the first record of this case carried a wrong number for an hour until the AI read it. Judgement: for a skill whose product is a conversation the graph could not verify the conversation, and earned its keep on the two things it could hold, the missing tests and the half-finished rename.
